# Brag Plan: NYC food + shelter alerts

## What is this app?
A service where NYC food banks and shelters text or call nearby people who said yes in person: "When a pantry has food, the neighbors hear first."

## The angle
A quiet, premium civic film. The product's strongest claim isn't speed, it's restraint: every alert is a person who said yes, and the rules say what it will never do. The video follows one alert (the site's own "Tuesday at a Brooklyn pantry" example) from the staff form, through the address check, to a neighbor's phone, then lands on the rules.

## Hook (0-4.2s)
Hero headline, typed on a dark navy field: "When a pantry has food," / "the neighbors hear first."

## Key moments
- New alert form: "Hot meals, 5 to 7 pm" typed, cap 60, Send alert clicked on a beat.
- "We check before sending": address checks out -> goes out; no match -> held for a person to review, nothing sent.
- A phone receives the text; STOP reply ends messages.
- Three "Never" rules arrive one by one.

## Outro / punchline
"Every alert is a person who said yes." + wordmark + honest "Prototype" note.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: staff open New alert -> key action: write message, cap 60, Send -> result: address checked, neighbor's phone gets the text.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium civic film
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, confident restraint; no jokes, warm yellow accent used sparingly.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 24s

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Text: #E4E9F0 (muted #A5B4CC), ok #62D39B, bad #FF8F86

## Share copy (draft)
When a pantry has food, the neighbors hear first. NYC food + shelter alerts: texts and calls only to people who said yes in person, and nothing goes out until the address checks out.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: bundled "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 9" (Sascha Ende, ende.app, CC BY 4.0), ~114.8 BPM
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume ~0.45, 0.6s fade-in, 1.6s fade-out to the end
- Music cue guidance: preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9...music-cues.md`; strong cues 4.23, 6.34, 8.44, 10.54, 12.65; beat grid 0.52s. Lock: form scene enters 4.23, Send click 8.44, check card 10.54.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (no extraction helper available in this environment; documented)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (keys while typing, click on Send, soft switch on card arrivals)
- Restraint rule: no risers, no hits on every beat.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 - Hook - 4.2s
Wordmark top-left; line 1 fades up; line 2 (yellow) lands on beat 2.12. Hold. Audio: bed in. Transition: soft fade -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - The pantry posts an alert - 5.3s (4.23-9.5)
New alert form card. Typing: address "214 Marcy Ave", message "Hot meals, 5 to 7 pm". Cap 60 with bar. Cursor clicks Send alert at 8.44. Sequential/interaction: yes (typing, click). Audio: keys, click. Transition: slide -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - We check before sending - 4.2s (9.5-13.7)
Heading left, two result cards right: "A page names the pantry and its street" -> goes out; "No page matches" -> "Held for a person to review. Nothing is sent." Sequential: yes (cards one by one). Transition: soft -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Neighbors hear first - 3.2s (13.7-16.9)
Phone: bubble "Hot meals, 5 to 7 pm"; reply "STOP"; caption "Reply STOP and the messages end." Transition: soft -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 - The rules - 3.7s (16.9-20.6)
"What this will never do" + three Never rows arriving on beats, then held together. Transition: fade -> Scene 6

### Scene 6 - Outro - 3.4s (20.6-24.0)
"Every alert is a person who said yes." wordmark, "Prototype. Not open to real providers yet." URL. Fade out with music.

**Music mood:** upbeat-warm, restrained
**Audio summary:** warm bed under the whole film with a few motion-matched UI sounds, fading out on the closer.
